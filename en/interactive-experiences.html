What we offer

Turning interaction into commercial value

At MADARI, we design interactive solutions that help companies present their brands and products in a more engaging, clearer way — at exhibitions and events, in marketing campaigns and product launches.

We connect the digital experience to the business objective, so interaction is never just entertainment: it becomes part of a strategy to attract the audience, introduce the product, capture leads and measure engagement.

Engage

Experiences designed to draw visitors to the brand and invite them to take part.

Experience

Products and services presented interactively, making them easier to understand and explore.

Lead Generation

Optional, consent-based mechanisms to capture leads in line with the campaign.

Measure

Indicators and reports that help you understand how the experience performed.

FAQ

Questions before you start

Does MADARI only make games?

No. We design interactive experiences built to achieve marketing and business goals; games can be one part of the solution.

Is the service suitable for international companies?

Yes — especially companies targeting the Saudi market or exhibiting at events in the Kingdom.

Can the service be used outside exhibitions?

Yes. We can develop solutions for marketing campaigns, product launches, training, product experiences and more.

Can the experience capture customer data?

Lead capture mechanisms can be designed within the project scope, in line with regulatory requirements and user consent.

Can you design an experience for a specific product?

Yes. Solutions can be tailored to the product, sector, audience and goal.

Does the service include delivering the whole exhibition?

Scope depends on the agreement and the project. Interactive services can be delivered as part of a wider project in coordination with exhibition and event services.
